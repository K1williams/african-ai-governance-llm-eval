# Official sources and provenance — reviewed 15 November 2026

**Rwanda — Primary PDF**: https://rura.rw/fileadmin/Documents/ICT/Laws/Rwanda_national_Artificial_intelligence_Policy.pdf

Official publication hosted by the Rwanda Utilities Regulatory Authority (RURA). Original ministry PDF (https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Policies/Artificial_Intelligence_Policy.pdf) returned HTTP 403 during the independent browser audit; the RURA-hosted PDF presents the same policy text for the reviewed sections. Relevant passages include the summary, six policy areas, recommendation 9, sectoral illustration, proposed RAIO and ethical guidelines. Ministry background: https://www.minict.gov.rw/ai-policy

**Kenya — Primary PDF**: https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-03/Kenya%20AI%20Strategy%202025%20-%202030.pdf

Official ministry strategy with March 2025 cover, executive summary, three pillars, four enablers, and proposed policy milestones. Government launch: https://www.ict.go.ke/index.php/kenyas-artificial-intelligence-ai-strategy-2025-2030-launched-kicc-nairobi . The earlier seed used a `/2025-06/National-AI-Strategy_IMPLEMENTATION.pdf` filename, which is also indexed by the government site as the March 2025 strategy. The canonical March file path is preferred for consistency; this is not a claim the original was false.

**Nigeria — Primary PDF**: https://ncair.nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/National-Artificial-Intelligence-Strategy-19092025.pdf

Government-hosted September 2025 strategy, including five pillars, 34 strategies, proposed ethics expert group, R&D fund, and governance framework. See especially the executive summary, pillar 4 / 5 and sections on risk and trade-offs.

## Review scope and reproducibility limitations

All three official document identities were checked through official PDF text exposed by an online index; priority claims were spot-checked against source passages. All 60 question texts were independently *desk-screened* for framing, not verified line by line against the full original PDFs. The 18 negative/uncertainty items have not received exhaustive corpus-level negative-evidence checks. The inspection is not external peer review, exhaustive page-by-page legal research, nor full local PDF annotation. The execution container cannot download these PDFs at the time of review; no PDF SHA-256 or page-excerpt integrity is asserted. Every one of the 60 oracle extracts must be locally verified against the downloaded source PDF, with approval recorded in the evidence review sheet before any inferential model study is run. A strategy's silence on later events never proves their non-occurrence.

The question set remains a small, purposive, AI-assisted instrument, not a validated benchmark. Read `docs/INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md` for findings and disposition of identified issues.
